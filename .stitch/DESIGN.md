# Design System for LINE&LOOP

## 1. Brand Identity
**LINE&LOOP** is a premium brand for handmade ladies' garments, made slowly. The brand identity blends modern minimalism with traditional crafting metaphors (tailoring, needle, thread).

## 2. Color Palette
- **Primary Accent (Crimson Red):** `#D61C22` - Used for buttons, active states, highlights, sale prices, and the brand's iconic "Save" badge.
- **Background (Dark Mode Theme):** `#000000` to `#1A1A1A` - Used for major section backgrounds (like header, hero, footer) to recreate the high-contrast premium feel of the logo.
- **Surface (Light Mode / Content areas):** `#FFFFFF` - Used for product grids and clean content sections to maintain high contrast.
- **Text Primary:** `#FFFFFF` (on dark backgrounds) or `#000000` (on light backgrounds).
- **Text Secondary:** `#666666` for descriptions and subtitles.

## 3. Typography
- **Headings:** Bold, geometric sans-serif (e.g., Inter, Montserrat, or system default sans) styled in **uppercase** with wide tracking (letter-spacing) to match the logo.
- **Body:** Clean, legible sans-serif for product details and journal entries. 
- **Style:** High contrast, sharp, and highly legible.

## 4. UI/UX Principles
- **Minimalism & Whitespace:** Generous spacing between sections and elements. Let the product photography and typography breathe.
- **High Contrast:** Sharp contrasts between deep charcoal backgrounds and pure white or vibrant red elements.
- **Premium Feel:** Sharp edges (or very subtle rounded corners like `ROUND_FOUR`), underline hover effects for links, and simple but elegant transitions.

## 5. Components
- **Top Announcement Bar:** Dark background with white, spaced-out small text.
- **Header:** Features the logo. Minimalist navigation links on the right.
- **Hero Section:** Dark charcoal/black gradient background, large uppercase heading, contrasting primary red CTA button ("Shop the Collection").
- **Product Cards:** Clean imagery, title, pricing. If an item is on sale or new, use a subtle tag in primary red.
- **Buttons:** Solid rectangles, primarily in `#D61C22` or `#1A1A1A`, with white text. No excessive drop shadows; flat and modern.

## 6. Design System Notes for Stitch Generation
**Color Mode:** `DARK`
**Font:** `INTER`
**Roundness:** `ROUND_FOUR`
**Custom Color:** `#D61C22`

The overall aesthetic must be high-end e-commerce, balancing modern dark-mode elements with ultra-clean white product grids. The red accent must be used tastefully for CTAs and highlights.
