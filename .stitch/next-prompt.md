A high-end 'About Us' (The Craft) page for a premium women's apparel brand called LINE&LOOP. It should feature a highly elegant, feminine luxury layout.

**DESIGN SYSTEM (REQUIRED):**
**Color Mode:** `DARK`
**Font:** `INTER`
**Roundness:** `ROUND_FOUR`
**Custom Color:** `#D61C22`

The overall aesthetic must be "Feminine Noir", balancing warm dark-mode elements (espresso/charcoal) with luxurious cream/ivory sections. The red accent must be used elegantly.

**Page Structure:**
1. **Header:** The global minimal header.
2. **Hero Story:** A full-width cinematic image of an artisan's hands, with a large, elegant serif-style headline reading "THE ART OF SLOW FASHION" centered over the image.
3. **Our Philosophy:** A split section. Left: a beautiful editorial photo of a linen garment. Right: A generous block of text explaining the brand's dedication to small-batch, hand-stitched garments in Pakistan.
4. **The Process (Timeline):** A visually stunning 3-step vertical or horizontal timeline: 
   - 01. Sourcing Natural Fibers
   - 02. The Hand-Dyeing Process
   - 03. Precision Stitching
5. **Founder Note:** A clean, cream-colored section featuring a quote from the founder about rejecting mass production in favor of heritage craft.
6. **Footer:** The global dark footer.
